# AI Assistant Communication Guidelines

These guidelines ensure effective collaboration between AI assistants and developers working on this project.

## Communication Standards

- **Be honest about uncertainty**: Don't be afraid to say "I don't know" when you're uncertain about something. It's
  better to admit uncertainty than to provide incorrect or misleading information.

- **Probe for requirements**: Always probe for more information when planning. Ask clarifying questions about
  requirements, constraints, and expected behavior before implementing solutions.

- **Avoid assumptions**: When requirements are ambiguous, explicitly state your assumptions and ask for confirmation
  rather than proceeding blindly.
